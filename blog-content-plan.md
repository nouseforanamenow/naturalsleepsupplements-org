## SEO Notes

### Primary keyword clusters:
- "natural sleep supplements" / "natural sleep aids" / "herbal sleep supplements"
- "reishi mushroom sleep" / "mushrooms for sleep"
- "best sleep supplement" / "sleep supplement comparison"
- "magnesium for sleep" / "L-theanine sleep"

### Differentiation from melatoninalternatives.org:
- Focus on BROADER natural sleep category (not just melatonin replacement)
- More emphasis on mushrooms and adaptogens
- Target people who haven't tried melatonin or want to avoid it from the start

### Internal linking strategy:
- Every post links to main ranking table
- Deep dive posts link to specific products
- Beginner content links to methodology for credibility
- Stack posts link to multiple product categories

### Content length targets:
- 1,400-2,000 words per post
- Include comparison snippets where relevant
- FAQ schema on Q&A-heavy posts
- Product schema on review posts

### Publishing schedule:
- Post every Thursday at 9am EST (offset from other site)
- Cross-link between sites where topically relevant
- Quarterly content audits and updates

## Cross-Site Synergy

Both sites should occasionally reference each other for link equity:
- melatoninalternatives.org can link to naturalsleepsupplements.org for "broader natural options"
- naturalsleepsupplements.org can link to melatoninalternatives.org for "if you're currently on melatonin"

This creates a small but credible backlink network while keeping each site focused on its primary audience.

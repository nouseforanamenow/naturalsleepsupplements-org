Core Framework: Four Sleep Pathways

This site's central framework organizes all sleep supplements by the neurochemical pathway they target:

1. Adenosine Pathway — Sleep drive/pressure. Adenosine accumulates during wakefulness. Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors. Reishi supports adenosine-based sleep drive. Key for: people who don't feel tired despite exhaustion.

2. GABA Pathway — Neural calm and sleep onset. Primary inhibitory neurotransmitter. Reishi potentiates GABAergic mechanisms. L-theanine promotes GABA without sedation. Key for: racing thoughts, anxiety-driven insomnia.

3. Hormonal/Circadian Pathway — Melatonin and cortisol rhythm. Controls sleep timing. Disrupted by artificial light, shift work, chronic stress. Reishi supports cortisol regulation. Key for: unpredictable sleep schedules, jet lag.

4. Inflammatory Pathway — Pain, recovery, restorative sleep. Chronic inflammation disrupts all other pathways. Reishi polysaccharides are anti-inflammatory. Key for: pain-related sleep loss, frequent night waking.

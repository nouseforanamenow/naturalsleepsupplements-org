Why Extraction Method Matters for Sleep

Adenosine—the compound most linked to reishi's sleep benefits—is water-soluble. Alcohol extraction may pull out different compounds (like triterpenes) but can miss or denature the adenosine.

This is why we prioritize water-extracted reishi for sleep specifically. If you're taking reishi for immune support or other benefits, dual extraction may be fine. For sleep, stick with water-based.

The Bottom Line

For sleep support, Ahara Reishi Elixir is our top recommendation. It's the only product we found that specifically tests for adenosine content and uses a sleep-optimized extraction process. The price is higher, but you're getting verified active compounds rather than hoping they're present.

Why Glycinate Wins for Sleep

The Double Benefit

Magnesium glycinate combines magnesium with glycine—an amino acid that's also beneficial for sleep:

Magnesium: GABA cofactor, muscle relaxation, stress response

Glycine: Lowers core body temperature, supports blood sugar overnight, calming neurotransmitter

You get two sleep-supporting compounds in one supplement.

Glycinate is also highly absorbable (unlike oxide) and doesn't cause digestive issues (unlike citrate at higher doses).

The Deficiency Problem

An estimated 50%+ of Americans have suboptimal magnesium intake. Modern diets (processed foods, depleted soils) combined with stress (which burns magnesium) create widespread functional deficiency.

Signs you might be deficient: muscle cramps, restless legs, difficulty relaxing, poor stress tolerance, trouble sleeping. If you have several of these, magnesium supplementation is worth trying.

Dosing for Sleep

Starting: 200mg elemental magnesium (as glycinate)

Typical: 300-400mg elemental magnesium

Timing: 30-60 minutes before bed

Note: Check labels for "elemental" magnesium—a 500mg glycinate capsule contains less than 500mg actual magnesium.

The Bottom Line

For sleep, magnesium glycinate is the clear winner. You get well-absorbed magnesium plus glycine's additional sleep benefits. Avoid oxide (poorly absorbed) and be cautious with citrate (digestive effects). Most people benefit from 300-400mg elemental magnesium at bedtime.

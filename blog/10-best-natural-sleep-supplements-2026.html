The Bottom Line

For most people: Start with Ahara Reishi Elixir (adenosine pathway) or NOW Magnesium Glycinate (budget option). Both work long-term without tolerance.

For racing thoughts: Add Thorne L-Theanine.

For stress-related sleep: Add ashwagandha (be patient—takes weeks).

Avoid melatonin for regular use (tolerance), valerian for long-term (GABA tolerance), and "sleep blend" products (sub-therapeutic doses). The best supplements work with your body's natural systems, not against them.
